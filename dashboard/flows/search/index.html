Search for messages in a flow

From the Dashboard's main navigation, focus on Your Flows, then select the desired flow

Your Flows Select a flow

Search for messages in a flow to find defects or check your integrations. Select one or more flows from the Select Flows to start.

  • Basic search
  • Advanced search

Use the top Search box to filter messages by property.

Message propertyDescription
Chain ID(= transaction ID). Also known as the 'client tracking ID' in Azure Logic Apps.
DSAVDomain, Service, Action, Version business properties.

Open the Advanced Search pane to add more filters to your query.

FilterDescription
StatusMessages at a given state:

  • Active (= messages in progress, resubmit & resume included)
  • Completed (= messages done)
  • Error (= failed messages, including Azure Logic Apps)
  • Ignored (= messages ignored by users)
  • Cancelled (= messages cancelled by users) v6.2

    Feature included since Invictus v6.2.

Date filterMessages in a given time frame:

  • All dates (= last 30 days)
  • After your date
  • Before your date
  • Between your date and your date
  • Last amount time period
DSAVFilter by mapping fields: Domain, Service, Action, Version. Invictus reads these from Azure Logic App tracking (see importing via Azure Logic Apps). Use Contains Word for partial matches.
Chain IDMessages with the same Chain ID. In Azure Logic Apps, this is the 'client tracking ID'.
PropertiesFilter messages by property:
  • your property Equal your value (= exact match)
  • your property Not Equal your value (= exclude matches)
  • your property Starts With your value (= prefix match)
  • your property Ends With your value (= suffix match)
  • your property Contains your value (= partial match)
  • your property Regex your value (= pattern match, learn more)
